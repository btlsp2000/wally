Night Prowlers & Pre-Dawn Acoustics LKA-WW-2026-09-26
SERIES: LKA / Route 2 • DATE: Saturday, September 26, 2026 • NODE: Wrightwood Library, CA (92397) • AUTHOR: Barstow Bill Tonnesen

At the Wrightwood Library this morning. While I sat engaged on the computer five feet away, I overheard a patron in concert with Robin, the veteran librarian, sharing night photos of bear, cougar, fox, and wildcat taken right at her home.

Guess I was right about setting that fallen tree limb ready at camp this morning—to wield in my right hand. The cougar will be the most dangerous, and they climb trees. A lot of trees at camp.

The crickets go silent when the temp drops early morning, and the owl takes over. Lovely sounds. I can't see the crickets or the owl.

The Beauty Salon LKA-1965-YOC
SERIES: LKA // Early Work Ledger • ERA: 1965 (Age 14) • NODE: Youth Opportunity Center (YOC) • AUTHOR: Barstow Bill Tonnesen

It was 1965, and the writing was on the wall. Dad was drinking and delinquent, and the household margin was gone. Mom looked at me and said it plain: "Get a job."

I went down to the YOC—the Youth Opportunity Center—and landed a Sunday gig at The Beauty Salon. While the rest of the neighborhood had the weekend off, Sunday became a workday—earning my own keep at fourteen before I even had a driver's license.
