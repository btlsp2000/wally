The Calculus of Pool

Vector Mechanics, Cut Angle Dynamics & Tangent Line Geometry

1. Ghost Ball Center Distance

The cut angle θ determines the lateral offset d between the aiming line and the object ball center:

d = 2R · sin(θ)

At a 30° cut angle, the center offset is exactly half the ball diameter (1.0 R).

2. The 90-Degree Tangent Law

For frictionless, non-rotating elastic collisions (stun shot), momentum conservation dictates that the post-collision trajectories form a right angle:

θcue + θobj = 90°

The object ball travels along the line of centers (Normal Vector), while the cue ball drifts along the Tangent Line.

3. Velocity Transfer Decomposition

Kinetic energy transfer along the line of impact varies as a function of the cut angle cosine:

Vobject = Vcue · cos(θ)

Thinner cuts transfer significantly less momentum to the object ball, retaining velocity in the cue ball tangent path.
