Route 2: The New Family Times

Las Vegas, NV • 36.1342° N, 115.1372° W November 19, 2026 Archive: /rtwo

BESET: Coordinates of the Siege

Surveying a perimeter under simultaneous structural, medical, and geographic strain.

When the siege begins, it does not arrive as a single catastrophic event; it arrives as an accumulation of severed lines and closed doors, plotted simultaneously across the grid of a life.

At the top of the ledger sits the irreversible loss: Mike is gone[cite: 2]. Losing a brother—the sibling who shared the exact same early coordinates—erases a living connection that cannot be reconstructed[cite: 2]. You look at the remaining points on the family line, your sons, and recognize a hard structural truth: two and three, with Wesley already gone, are not substitutes[cite: 2]. Each node holds its own distinct position; one cannot simply be shifted over to bear the load of another, especially when the friction of their own presence—or absence—only sharpens the isolation[cite: 2].

At the same time, the physical frame itself gives way. Emergency surgery, a diagnosis of heart failure, and now the shadow of cancer suspicions—marked by the diagnostics at SimonMed Imaging on South Maryland Parkway—turn the body from a reliable instrument into an uncertain variable[cite: 1, 2]. When the foundation is compromised, every external stress multiplies.

So you relocate, shifting coordinates to Las Vegas, only to find the perimeter tightening[cite: 2]. Behind you, the familiar anchor points are either locked or out of reach: Coquille is closed, San Diego is closed, winter has descended on Wrightwood, and both Oregon and Trinity County sit too far across the map to offer immediate shelter[cite: 2].

Even the local ground offers resistance—River Park withholding a deposit return, the petty friction of Heritage Park antics, the immediate physical toll of failing teeth, and the bureaucratic maze of resetting with new insurance and new doctors[cite: 2].

Layered over the personal ledger is the broader static: mounting debt and the background hum of global turmoil[cite: 2]. Everything converges on a single vertical axis[cite: 2]. You are no longer drafting a blueprint for expansion; you are surveying a perimeter under pressure, mapping every point of strain just to keep the structure standing.
