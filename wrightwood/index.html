#2-WEEK

Operational Distinction: 2-WEEK functions as a mobile, 14-day touring facilitator and field logistics resource—intentionally decoupled from the permanent/semi-permanent home base at Carson Suites in Downtown Las Vegas, NV. Designed for self-contained multi-modal transit circuits (bus, rail, air, and foot) across high-desert and mountain corridors such as Wrightwood (WWD), Victor Valley, Southern California, and Oregon.
