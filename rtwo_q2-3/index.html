The Clean Room & The Thanksgiving Turkey

APT 219, CARSON SUITES • NOV 25, 2026

I sit in Apartment 219 at Carson Suites on the morning before Thanksgiving Day, 2026. Outside the door and inside the bathroom cupboards, there are no roaches anywhere.

Mike is gone. Wesley is gone. Looking back to Vista Garden (“Cockroach Heaven”) and to the Thanksgiving when Mom (Betty Jo) pulled a full turkey from the oven while Lydia sat quietly aghast—you realize selective compliance held the theater together.

“What could I say?” Now the table is cleared, the cupboards are clean, and Apt 219 holds its own peace.
