6.0 The Degree of Difficulty Matrix

The geometric physics of the table dictate only five primary shot classifications: Cut, Carom, Combination, Kick, and Bank.

The Bank Theorem: Amateurs view the bank as an inherent liability. However, mathematically, many times the bank is optimal. By minimizing positional variance ($P$) and allowing the cue ball to travel on a natural post-collision tangent line, it becomes the least error-prone path and the best option for securing the next coordinate.

To quantify this, the Degree of Difficulty ($\theta$) is calculated not as a product, but as a Cartesian vector magnitude: ƒ(S,P) → θ = √(S² + P²).
