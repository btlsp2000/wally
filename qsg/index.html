WallyCAD Quick Start Guide

Hey Jason,

Let's get your feet wet in WallyCAD (wallycad.com).

1. Layout Overview

  • 📌 Top Menu / Functions: Quick-access command buttons (like WALL).
  • 📐 Work Area (Canvas): The main interactive drawing space.
  • 🗂️ Sidebar / Input Panel: Handles parameters, layer references (CL), and data controls.

2. Quick Start Steps

  1. Open wallycad.com.
  2. Click the "WALL" command button at the top menu bar.
  3. In the sidebar panel, click the "Export/Import (Wally)" button.

3. Next Step to Try

Once your workspace is open and your data is loaded, try clicking a starting point on the canvas to plot your first wall segment, or check the sidebar input fields to adjust dimensions.
