The Family Times: Route 2

August 2026

Welcome to the latest edition of the family updates. The wheels keep turning, and this chapter brings us down to Barstow with plenty of fresh air, open roads, and new projects on the drafting table.

On the Road and Building Out

The desert sun here in Barstow has been an excellent backdrop for recent travels, especially while soaking in the history of the Route 66 Centennial Caravan. Between wrapping up the move from the Bristol apartment and hitting the road, the mobile lifestyle remains as busy and rewarding as ever.

  • The Space Trailer: The custom cabin "Trailer-Mini" build is making steady progress. Ironing out the chassis specifications, Warren truss details, and stair winder geometry has been a great way to blend structural detailing with life on the move.
  • Library Stops: Local libraries—from Gresham down to Hesperia, Wrightwood, and now Barstow—continue to serve as the perfect quiet outposts for reading, studying, and writing code.

Digital Drafts and Table Geometry

It hasn’t just been physical builds; the software and writing projects are moving at full speed.

  • WallyCAD: The AutoLISP and JavaScript routines are running smoothly, and it's been exciting to see the architectural offset and miter tools out there on Gumroad and GitHub.
  • QuickClick (QC): The matrix-based shorthand keyboard interface is coming together nicely, making the drafting process faster and more intuitive.
  • The Calculus of Pool: The Tangent Lab manual is expanding. Benchmarking the collision dynamics, cut angles, and multi-rail kick shot systems is an ongoing puzzle—there's always a new vector to map out when the cue ball hits the felt.
  • Where is Bill?: The board game mechanics and location spinners are really taking shape, perfectly capturing the regional transit and travel themes.
